Attribution: last-touch; 30-day window for in-product triggers, 7-day for email clicks. Cohort is snapshot at trial start. Lift shown only when fired and not-fired groups both have ≥ 10 tenants. Row-level security: CSM leads see drill-down rows only for tenants they own; read-only sees aggregates.
