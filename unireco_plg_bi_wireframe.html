Widget 2 rules

Rule
Rowsone per trial_id with gate_lifted, inside the start-date range and cohort filter; a Widget 1 cell click narrows to tenants at or past that stage
ColumnsDay 1 … Day 30; Day 1 = the day the gate lifted
Cell contentevery event whose trial_day equals the column, as chips in order: Login · Landed · Used Returns · Creds added · Used SPF · I.x · Converted · Expired. The same event repeated on one day is shown once
Current daythe column where trial_day = today, tinted light green; only for active trials
Lapsed dayscolumns before the current day, grey. Upcoming days white
After conversioncolumns after the Converted day tinted green and labelled Live; the row keeps its position
After day 30no conversion → Expired chip on day 30, row status Expired, all columns hatched
Status columnActive · day N  |  Inactive · Nd (no login in the last 5 trial days)  |  Converted · day N  |  Expired
ReconciliationWidget 1 and Widget 2 are the same query with different aggregation: under the same filters, Widget 1's Landed count equals Widget 2's row count
Refresh & access15 minutes. Product/PLG and Business Head: all rows. CSM leads: rows for tenants they own. Read-only: Widget 1 only
